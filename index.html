About Protec

Professional judgement.
Practical outcomes.

Protec Fire Safety Ltd is an independent UK consultancy helping dutyholders, property professionals and organisations understand and manage fire safety risk.

Led by Director and assessor Jeff Hall, our work is grounded in recognised professional competence and focused on clear, proportionate recommendations that clients can act upon.
